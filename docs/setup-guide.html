Setup guide · about 20 minutes

Your WhatsApp assistant, from nothing to answering customers

Seven steps. You will copy and paste two commands, scan one QR code, and write a few sentences about your business. No coding, and nothing to install on your own computer.

runs on your own server your data stays with you answers only people who message you

What you are setting up

A customer messages your WhatsApp number. The assistant reads it, answers in your words, and sounds like a person on your team — short replies, all lower case, with a natural pause before answering. If nobody follows up, it sends one or two gentle nudges and then stops.

hi, are you open today?
yep, till 5 today seen · replied 14 seconds later
do you deliver to clifton?
we do, it's £5 anywhere in town
perfect, i'll come by instead
no problem, see you soon!

The moment you reply to someone yourself from your phone, the assistant goes quiet in that chat permanently. It will never talk over you.

Before you start

Four things. Gather them now and the rest takes twenty minutes.

  • A spare phone number with WhatsApp on it. Not your personal number — see the warning at the bottom of this page.
  • A server. Any small VPS from Hostinger, DigitalOcean, Hetzner or similar, running Ubuntu. The cheapest one is plenty.
  • A web address you control, so you can use a subdomain like chat.yourbusiness.com.
  • An OpenRouter account with a few pounds of credit, from openrouter.ai. This is what powers the replies, and you pay them directly for what you use.
1

Point your web address at the server

3 minutes, then a short wait

Sign in wherever you bought your domain and find the DNS records screen. Add one record:

TypeNamePoints to
Achatyour server's IP address
The one thing people get wrong

In the Name box type only chat — not chat.yourbusiness.com. Your provider adds the rest. Typing the full address creates something that will never work.

Save it, then wait two or three minutes before the next step.

2

Install it with one command

3 minutes

Open your server's terminal. Every host has a button for this — Hostinger calls it Browser terminal, others call it Console. Paste this line, replacing the address with your own, and press Enter.

your server 
# curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/meris01/what-sapp-agent/main/scripts/bootstrap.sh | bash -s chat.yourbusiness.com

5

Add your OpenRouter key

2 minutes

This is what writes the replies. Go to openrouter.ai/keys, create a key, and copy it. Then in the dashboard open Settings, paste it into OpenRouter API key, and press Save.

A sensible model is already chosen (openai/gpt-5.6-luna). Leave it unless you have a reason to change it. Press Test connection and wait for the green tick.

What this costs

You pay OpenRouter directly, per reply. For a small shop answering a few dozen messages a day this is typically well under a pound a week. Top up your balance there, not here.

6

Tell it about your business

10 minutes, and worth taking your time

Open Instructions. This is the only part that is really yours to write, and it decides how good the assistant is. There is a Insert a starting template button if you want a skeleton to fill in.

a good example 
you are the assistant for Rossi's Pizzeria in Bristol.

what we offer
- pizzas from £9 to £14, see the menu at rossis.co.uk/menu
- gluten free bases, £2 extra

practical details
- open tuesday to sunday, 5pm to 10pm. closed mondays.
- 12 park street, bristol
- delivery within 3 miles, £3, usually 40 minutes

how to handle customers
- answer questions about the list above. anything else,
  say you will check with the team.
- never promise a discount or a delivery time that is
  not written here.
- to take an order, collect the items, name and address,
  then say someone will ring to confirm.
- if someone is unhappy, apologise briefly and say a
  colleague will reply here shortly.

Press Save, then Test instructions to see a sample reply without anything being sent to WhatsApp.

Already handled for you

Short lower-case texting style, the natural pause before replying, matching the customer's language, and refusing to invent prices. You only need the facts about your business.

1 minute

Use a different phone

A message sent from the linked phone itself counts as you taking over, which switches the assistant off for that chat permanently. That is deliberate — just not what you want mid-test.

That is everything. It now runs on its own, including after a reboot.

Living with it

Taking over a conversation

Just reply from your phone as normal. The assistant stops in that chat immediately and for good — even if it was halfway through writing something. Nothing to switch off.

Pausing everything

Settings → Automation → turn it off. Messages still arrive and are recorded, but nothing is answered and nothing is marked as read.

When a customer says "stop"

Handled automatically. They get one short acknowledgement and are never messaged again. This is a legal requirement in most places, so it cannot be turned off.

Follow-ups

If a customer goes quiet after the assistant replies, it nudges them twice — after three hours, then the next day — and then stops. Held back overnight so nobody is messaged at 3am. Change the number and the timing under Settings → Follow-ups.

Adding your staff

Team → Invite a member gives you a single-use link to send them. They pick their own password. There is no open sign-up, on purpose: anyone with an account can read every customer conversation.

It remembers your customers

Names, what they wanted, their address, what was agreed. Someone who messages again next month will not be asked the same questions twice.

If something goes wrong

What you seeWhat to do
The page will not load at all DNS has not caught up. Wait five minutes and try again. If it still fails, check the record's Name box says only chat.
"Incorrect username or password" The username is admin — easy to mistype. The password is in /opt/whatsapp-agent/.env on your server.
The QR code will not scan Press Refresh connection for a fresh one. They expire after about a minute.
It stopped replying to one person Almost always because you replied to them yourself, or they said "stop". Both are permanent by design.
It stopped replying to everyone Check Settings — usually automation was paused, or the OpenRouter balance ran out.
Says "Disconnected" WhatsApp dropped the link. It reconnects on its own; if not, scan the QR again.

To restart it, or to see what it has been doing:

your server 
# systemctl restart whatsapp-agent
# journalctl -u whatsapp-agent -f

Read this before you use a number that matters

Use a spare number

This connects to WhatsApp in a way WhatsApp's terms do not permit. In practice, low-volume inbound answering like this is rarely troubled — but the number can be restricted or banned, without warning and with no reliable appeal, and no amount of settings changes that.

Use a number your business could operate without. Not your personal number, and not the one printed on your van.

Two other things worth knowing. Replies are written by an AI, so review conversations now and again — you are responsible for what your business says. And customer conversations are stored on your server, which makes you responsible for keeping it secure and for telling customers that enquiries are handled with AI assistance.

If you would rather have none of that risk, there is an official paid WhatsApp Business API that this can be moved onto. Ask whoever sold you this.
